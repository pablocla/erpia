# Prompt Generador de Video (Google Veo / Luma / Runway)

**Objetivo:** Generar un video hiper-realista para TikTok/Reels que fomente la creación de "Sedes" (agencias de ventas) de Claver ERP, apelando al estilo de vida, negocios, tecnología y ganancias pasivas.

## Prompt para IA (Copia y Pega)

> **Visuals:** Cinematic, vertical format (9:16), shot on RED V-Raptor, 8k resolution, photorealistic.
> 
> **Scene 1 (0-3s):** A dynamic, fast-paced pan over a sleek, modern smartphone screen displaying a futuristic dashboard with a massive upward green profit chart and a notification that reads "$100,000 Depositado". The screen glows slightly in a dimly lit, luxurious modern cafe.
>
> **Scene 2 (3-6s):** Cut to a confident, young entrepreneur in casual smart clothing (a black turtleneck) holding the phone, looking directly into the camera with a subtle, knowing smile. Background is slightly blurred (bokeh) showing an expensive sports car parked outside the window.
>
> **Scene 3 (6-9s):** A fast montage: Hands typing quickly on a glowing laptop showing the Claver ERP logo, a handshake between two people in suits, and a digital network of glowing nodes expanding across a map of Argentina, symbolizing the "Sedes" (affiliate network) growing exponentially.
>
> **Scene 4 (9-12s):** Text overlay appears with bold, neon-white typography with a glassmorphism effect: "TU PROPIA AGENCIA DE SOFTWARE". The camera zooms out rapidly to reveal the person standing in a high-rise office overlooking the city at sunset. 
>
> **Lighting & Mood:** High contrast, neon teal and subtle orange accents, fast-paced, highly aspirational, "tech-bro" meets Wall Street luxury.
