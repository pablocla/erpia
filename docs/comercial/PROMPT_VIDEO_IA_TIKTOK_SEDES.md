# Prompt Generador de Video (Google Veo / Luma / Runway)

**Objetivo:** Generar un video hiper-realista para TikTok/Reels que fomente la creación de "Sedes" (agencias de ventas) de Claver ERP. Debe mostrar tanto el estilo de vida de ingresos pasivos como el **valor real del software** que van a vender (Punto de venta, Inteligencia Artificial, Inventario).

## Prompt para IA (Copia y Pega)

> **Visuals:** Cinematic, vertical format (9:16), shot on RED V-Raptor, 8k resolution, photorealistic.
> 
> **Scene 1 (0-3s):** A dynamic, fast-paced pan over a sleek, modern smartphone screen displaying a futuristic dashboard. The screen shows two things: a modern Point of Sale (POS) interface with beautiful graphs, and a notification popping up that reads "$100,000 Depositado". The screen glows slightly in a dimly lit, luxurious modern cafe.
>
> **Scene 2 (3-6s):** Cut to a confident, young entrepreneur in casual smart clothing holding the phone, looking directly into the camera with a subtle, knowing smile. A blurred background (bokeh) shows a busy, aesthetic retail store where a cashier is happily using an iPad with the same Claver ERP interface to ring up a customer.
>
> **Scene 3 (6-9s):** A fast, high-energy montage: A close-up of a glowing laptop screen showing a futuristic AI assistant (Claver IA) analyzing inventory; a person tapping a smartphone on a terminal to pay; a digital network of glowing nodes expanding across a map of Argentina, symbolizing the "Sedes" (affiliate network) growing exponentially.
>
> **Scene 4 (9-12s):** Text overlay appears with bold, neon-white typography with a glassmorphism effect: "TU PROPIA AGENCIA DE SOFTWARE". The camera zooms out rapidly to reveal the person standing in a high-rise office overlooking the city at sunset.
>
> **Lighting & Mood:** High contrast, neon teal and subtle orange accents, fast-paced, highly aspirational, "tech-bro" meets Wall Street luxury but grounded in real business solutions.
